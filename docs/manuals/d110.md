The manual's controls are the power button and the indicator.

## Indicator

The manual lists `Синий` (blue) and `Красный` (red) and nothing else — **there is no green row**. Under Bluetooth, the pairing password is `0000` or `1234` if one is asked for.

| Colour | Pattern | Manual's stated meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Blue | blinking | Bluetooth disconnected |
| Blue | fast blinking | printing or charging |
| Blue | steady | Bluetooth connected, or the battery is full |
| Red | blinking | Bluetooth disconnected, or the battery is low |
| Red | fast blinking | printing, charging, or the battery is low |
| Red | steady | Bluetooth connected, or the battery is low |

The blue and red rows overlap in the manual, so treat the table as what the manual claims rather than as a clean state machine: the same pattern appears under two conditions in both colours. It is a reason to check the app's own status before believing a colour, not a code to decode.

## Troubleshooting

The manual's questions and answers, in its own order:

| Symptom | Manual's answer |
|---|---|
| Will not fully charge from a laptop, so it switches off | The laptop's USB output is not enough. Charge from a wall socket. |
| PIN `0000` or `1234` is refused | The device may not be recognised by the app yet. Enter `0` sixteen times to refresh, then try the PIN again. |
| Prints normally but the sheet comes out blank | The paper may be loaded the wrong way round. |
| Text is grey or faint | More labels were printed than were set, or the chip is faulty. |
| "Use a new version of the paper" | Only original paper is supported. It may be a momentary chip-detection failure, in which case ask support. It may be a Bluetooth fault, in which case restart the phone and Bluetooth. |
| The app cannot find the printer | Unplug it if it is charging and search again. Bluetooth is slow: reopen the app and retry. Update or reinstall the app. |
| Does it support batch printing, and how many? | Yes. The app allows up to 999 labels in one batch, but with the label stock's real length, no more than 300 labels per batch. |

## Reaching it over Bluetooth

The D110 advertises Bluetooth 4.0, and the printer presents two addresses: one low energy and one classic. The low energy side is reached through the characteristic `bef8d6c9-9c21-4c9e-b632-bd58c1009f9f` of service `e7810a71-73ae-499d-8c15-faa9aef0c3f2`; the classic side is a Bluetooth serial port that can fragment a packet across reads. See [the community wiki's connection page](https://printers.niim.blue/interfacing/connecting/) for that pairing and [its D110 page](https://printers.niim.blue/hardware/niimbot-d110/) for the model's own numbers.

A D110 that answers a scan without the serial port profile UUID is the low energy side only, so a Classic RFCOMM channel will not reach it. `POST /api/devices/scan` reports that as `"spp": false` next to a `"le"` service.
